## Standard Engine Overview

**6.2L V8 with Dynamic Fuel Management:**
The Escalade delivers impressive performance with a 6.2L V8 engine producing 420 HP and 623.7 Nm of torque. The standard Dynamic Fuel Management system varies the number of active cylinders, helping to optimize efficiency while maintaining the power needed for luxury SUV performance.

**Availability:** All trims except V-Series
**Technology:** Active Fuel Management for enhanced efficiency
**Fuel Type:** Premium gasoline recommended for optimal performance

---

## V-Series Engine Overview

**6.2L Supercharged V8 (Hand-Built):**
Exclusive to V-Series, this available ferocious-output engine continues the legacy of legendary Cadillac craftsmanship and performance. The hand-built supercharged V8 produces 682 HP and 885 Nm of torque for ultimate luxury SUV performance.

**Power Output:** 682 hp
**Torque:** 885 Nm
**Drivetrain:** AWD with single-speed transfer case
**Special Features:** Hand-built in Bowling Green, Kentucky for exclusivity and precision
