## Vehicle Overview

The 2025 Cadillac XT6 is a luxury mid-size SUV featuring 7-passenger seating as standard, a maximum cargo space of 2,229 liters behind the first row, and an available full-color head-up display. This three-row luxury SUV represents the perfect solution for families seeking sophisticated luxury with advanced technology.
